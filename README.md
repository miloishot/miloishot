### What I'm building

**[Invoco](https://invoco.org)** — a cloud POS for F&B in Singapore and Malaysia, built landlord-first: the operator who owns the food court gets one view across every stall, and payments are part of the product rather than an integration someone else owns. Multi-tenant Postgres with row-level security front to back, edge functions, self-hosted.

Most of it lives in private repos. What's below is the part that made sense to open up.
